CRF feature ablation analysis\n==================================================\nA_state_only: test_token=0.923, test_sentence=0.667\nB_transition_only: test_token=0.692, test_sentence=0.667\nC_full: test_token=1.000, test_sentence=1.000\nD_no_suffix: test_token=0.846, test_sentence=0.667\n\nInterpretation:\n1. State features provide observation-to-label evidence.\n2. Transition-only performance is lower because sequence patterns cannot identify words by themselves.\n3. In this toy dataset the full feature set performs best, showing the complementary role of state and transition information.\n4. Removing suffix/length features reduces test accuracy in this small experiment.\n5. These numbers demonstrate CRF mechanics; they are not a production Chinese POS benchmark.\n
